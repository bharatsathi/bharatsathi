About BharatSathi

BharatSathi is an independent digital platform created to make everyday digital tasks and useful information easier to access for people across India.

Our aim is to provide simple, easy-to-use online tools, educational resources, financial calculators and practical guides in English, Hindi and Marathi.

What you can find here

  • Free everyday calculators and productivity tools.
  • Student and career-related information.
  • Simple guides to help users understand digital services.
  • Financial calculators for learning and planning.

Our commitment

We work to keep information clear and useful. However, information may change, and users should verify important details with the relevant official organisation before taking action.
